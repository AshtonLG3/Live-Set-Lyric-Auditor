Studio lyrics are easy to find. Live lyrics are harder. Artists skip lines, change words, repeat hooks, and call out the city. Live-Set Lyric Auditor turns those moments into reviewable evidence.

Start with an authorized concert clip, a phone recording, or even a lyric fragment someone remembers after the show. The app builds a Live Variant Passport: a timestamped record of how the live performance compares with the canonical song.

Here the app is running in API mode. Musixmatch Pro anchors the recording with track search, common-track metadata, ISRC, lyric availability, subtitles, and RichSync when it is available.

Recall Rescue covers the messy case where the reviewer does not know the track. A typed, spoken, or sung fragment can still resolve a defensible Musixmatch anchor before analysis continues.

Once analysis starts, the pipeline validates the source, isolates the live vocal, profiles the arrangement, transcribes the clip, matches the recording, compares timing, and assembles the Passport.

The main review surface is Live vs Studio. Each live line sits beside its studio reference, with changed words, skipped lines, live-only ad libs, timing drift, and approve or reject decisions in one place.

The Passport also carries context. JamBase ties the result to the event, venue, lineup, and setlist position. Cyanite adds performance signals like energy, tempo, mood, and instrumentation.

At the end, the reviewer exports JSON with variants, timestamps, confidence, source mode, rights notes, and review decisions. The studio lyric says what was written. Live-Set Lyric Auditor shows what was actually heard.
